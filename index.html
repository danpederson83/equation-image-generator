Getting it into Google Slides

  1. Fastest: click Copy image, then click in your slide and press Ctrl+V (⌘+V on Mac).
  2. Or: click Download PNG, then in Slides choose Insert → Image → Upload from computer.
  3. Google Slides doesn't accept SVG files, so use the PNG there. The PNG is rendered at high resolution with a transparent background, so it stays sharp when you resize it.
  4. The SVG download is for apps that support it (PowerPoint, Keynote, Word, Canva, websites).
